Pulse - product telemetry

Pulse ingests events from your services and front ends, models funnels and retention automatically, and alerts when a release regresses a key journey. SDKs for TypeScript, Python, Go and Java; a streaming API; and warehouse sync in both directions.

Atlas - query engine

Atlas is the query layer under Pulse: columnar storage, a cost-based query planner and sub-second answers over billions of events. Teams use Atlas directly when they want SQL power with product semantics.

Platform

SSO, audit logs, regional data residency and a public API tie the products together. Built and operated by the customer platform squad.
