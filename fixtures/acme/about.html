Our story

Acme Analytics was founded in 2019 by three infrastructure engineers who were tired of shipping blind. Every release went out with logging, dashboards and hope - and nobody could answer the simplest question: did this change make the product better?

We built the tool we wanted: honest telemetry, fast queries, and no data science degree required. Today Acme Analytics is the analytics backbone for over 1,200 engineering teams.

How we work

We are remote-first and asynchronous by default. Decisions live in writing, our values are public, and the way we operate - from on-call to pricing - is documented openly in the company handbook. Get to know the people on the team page.
