We hire a handful of people every quarter and we would rather wait than lower the bar.

How we hire

Every engineering role follows the same published process. No puzzles, no trick questions:

  1. Intro call (30 min). You talk to the hiring manager about your experience and what you want next. We answer every question about pay and process honestly.
  2. Take-home exercise (up to 4 hours). A small, realistic slice of our real work: build a small service or feature with TypeScript, with tests and a short README. You get a full week to fit it into your life.
  3. System design round (60 min). We discuss your take-home, then design a scaled-up version of it together: data model, failure modes, trade-offs. There is no single right answer; we care about how you think.
  4. Values interview (45 min). A conversation with two future teammates about ownership, feedback and collaboration - grounded in the handbook values.
  5. Offer. Decisions within five working days of the final interview.

We pay for the take-home time if you ask, we give feedback to every finalist, and we never quiz people on algorithms they would google at work.

What we offer

  • Remote-first, async-by-default culture (see the handbook)
  • Salary bands published in the handbook, reviewed yearly
  • Four-day release cadence - you will ship in week one
  • Annual learning budget and a real on-call compensation policy
