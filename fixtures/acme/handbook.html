Our values

  • Ship to learn. Small releases beat big plans.
  • Write it down. If it is not written, it did not happen.
  • Own the outcome. We hire people who stay with a problem past the first fix.
  • Assume good intent. Especially in code review.

How engineering works

Squads of four to six engineers own slices of the platform end to end: Pulse ingestion, Atlas query planning, the SDK family, and the customer platform. Every service is TypeScript on Node.js with PostgreSQL and Kafka. We practice trunk-based development, review every change, and deploy dozens of times a day.

On-call is one week in eight, compensated, and nobody is paged for anything a robot can fix.
